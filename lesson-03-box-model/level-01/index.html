Intro to the Box Model

Read the instructions for this level in instructions.md.

The box model center piece for content(pictures or text). Padding is the cushion or space around the content. Both the content and padding are protected by the border. Border seals in the content and padding. Margin provides more space outside of the border.

Your notes

The box model has four layers with the content (images or text) getting the center attention. Padding is right on top of the content. After padding is the border which bascially put a protective shield around the content and padding. Last is the margin which puts space on every side out of the border.
